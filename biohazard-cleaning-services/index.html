Biohazard cleaning in Birmingham

Safe, discreet clean-up of blood, bodily fluids, human waste and other contaminated materials, across Birmingham and the West Midlands.

Call 0121 000 0000 Request a free quote

Biohazard cleaning is the specialist removal and disinfection of anything that could carry infection or cause harm: blood and bodily fluids, human waste, sharps, contaminated waste and the residue left behind after traumatic events. It is not something a household cleaner, or a bottle of bleach, can safely deal with.

We connect you with experienced specialist teams in Birmingham who wear the right protective equipment, use hospital-grade disinfectants and dispose of contaminated waste through the proper channels. Whether you are a homeowner, a family member, a landlord or a housing officer, you will get a clear quote and a calm, professional service.

What is included

  • Assessment of the contamination and a clear, no-obligation quote
  • Removal and bagging of contaminated materials, furniture and flooring where needed
  • Cleaning and disinfection of all affected surfaces
  • Odour treatment once the source has been removed
  • Transport and disposal of hazardous waste by a registered waste carrier
  • Final check so you can be confident the area is safe to use again

When you might need biohazard cleaning

  • After a death, particularly if the person was not found for some time
  • After an accident, assault or other traumatic incident at a property
  • Where there is human waste, vomit or sewage contamination
  • Following a hoarding or severe neglect situation
  • After a cannabis farm, squat or drug use has left a property contaminated
  • Where needles or sharps have been left in a home, garden or communal area

Biohazard Cleaning FAQs

Can I clean up biohazards myself?

It is strongly advised not to. Blood and bodily fluids can carry blood-borne viruses and bacteria, and household products do not disinfect to the standard needed. Contaminated waste also has to be disposed of through a registered carrier. A specialist team has the protective kit, products and licences to do it safely.

How much does biohazard cleaning cost?

It depends on the type and extent of contamination, the size of the area and how much has to be removed. We provide a free, no-obligation quote, and photographs often help us give you a realistic price quickly.

Is biohazard cleaning covered by insurance?

Sometimes. Some home and landlord policies contribute to the cost, particularly after a death or damage event. Check your policy wording or speak to your insurer, and ask for an itemised quote you can pass on.
