Crime scene and trauma cleaning in Birmingham

Discreet, thorough clean-up of blood and bodily fluids after an assault, accident or other traumatic incident.

Call 0121 000 0000 Request a free quote

After a violent incident, serious accident or sudden death, a property can be left with blood and bodily fluids on floors, walls and furnishings. It is distressing to see, and it is a genuine health risk. Police do not clean the scene, and the responsibility falls to the owner, tenant or landlord.

The teams we connect you with are used to working at sensitive scenes. They work quietly, keep your details private and restore the area to a clean, safe standard so you can start to move on.

What is included

  • Safe removal of blood, bodily fluids and contaminated materials
  • Cleaning and disinfection of walls, floors, fixtures and furnishings
  • Removal and replacement advice for items that cannot be saved
  • Odour treatment where needed
  • Licensed disposal of all biohazardous waste
  • Discreet working, with no unnecessary attention

When this service applies

  • After an assault, stabbing or other violent incident at a home or business
  • After a serious accident that has left blood or bodily fluids
  • After a sudden or traumatic death
  • Where the police have finished their investigation and released the property

Please do not try to clean a trauma scene yourself. Blood and bodily fluids can carry blood-borne infections, and some stains soak far deeper than they appear. If you need someone to talk to after a distressing event, the Samaritans are available on 116 123, any time.

Crime Scene & Trauma Cleaning FAQs

Can you attend before the police have finished?

No. Cleaning only begins once the police have released the property. If you are unsure whether it has been released, ask the officer in charge.

Will the clean-up be discreet?

Discretion is a priority. Teams keep the nature of the work private and avoid drawing attention to the property.

Can you help commercial premises as well as homes?

Yes. The specialist teams work with both domestic and commercial properties, including shops, offices and rented properties.
