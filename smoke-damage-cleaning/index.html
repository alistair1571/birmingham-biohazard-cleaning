Smoke damage cleaning in Birmingham

Specialist removal of smoke residue and smoke odour from homes and commercial properties after a fire.

Call 0121 000 0000 Request a free quote

Smoke does not stay where the fire was. It drifts through rooms, into cupboards and soft furnishings, and leaves a film of residue and a sharp, stubborn smell. Opening the windows and using air fresheners rarely solves it.

The teams we connect you with clean smoke residue from surfaces, treat soft furnishings and carpets, and use odour-neutralising treatments to remove the smell rather than mask it. They work in homes, rented properties and commercial premises.

What is included

  • Cleaning of walls, ceilings, floors and fixtures affected by smoke
  • Treatment of carpets, curtains and soft furnishings
  • Odour-neutralising treatment of the affected rooms
  • Cleaning of belongings that can be restored
  • Disposal of items that cannot be cleaned
  • A final check that the smell has gone

Common causes

  • A small kitchen fire or burnt cooking
  • A fire in a neighbouring flat or property
  • Electrical faults and overheating appliances
  • Chimney, fireplace or wood burner problems
  • Smoke from a fire elsewhere in the building

Avoid wiping soot with a damp cloth, as it can smear and stain surfaces. If you are unsure, leave it for a professional to treat.

Smoke Damage Cleaning FAQs

Will the smoke smell definitely go?

In most cases it can be removed or greatly reduced, provided the residue is cleaned and the source of the odour is treated. Heavily affected soft furnishings may need replacing.

Do I need a specialist, or will a normal clean do?

Smoke residue behaves differently to ordinary dirt and can smear if cleaned incorrectly. A specialist uses the right products and methods and treats odour at the source.

Does insurance cover smoke damage?

Often, if it results from an insured event such as a fire. Check your policy and speak to your insurer before work starts.
