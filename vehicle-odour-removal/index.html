Vehicle odour removal in Birmingham

Deep cleaning and odour treatment for cars, vans and taxis affected by smoke, spills, damp, pets and other lingering smells.

Call 0121 000 0000 Request a free quote

Smells in a vehicle are trapped by upholstery, carpets, the headlining and the ventilation system, and they come back after a quick wipe or an air freshener. Smoke, spilt food or drink, pet accidents, damp and bodily fluids can all leave a smell that is hard to live with.

The teams we connect you with deep clean the interior, treat the upholstery and carpets and use an odour-neutralising treatment to deal with the source of the smell. They work on private cars, vans, taxis and fleet vehicles.

What is included

  • Deep clean of seats, carpets, mats, headlining and trim
  • Treatment of the source of the smell
  • Odour-neutralising treatment of the cabin
  • Advice on cabin filters and ventilation
  • A final check before the vehicle is returned

Typical problems

  • Cigarette and vape smoke
  • Spilt food, drink and milk
  • Pet accidents and hair
  • Damp and mould smells
  • Bodily fluids, vomit or other contamination in a vehicle

Vehicle Odour Removal FAQs

Can the smell be removed completely?

In most cases it can, once the source has been found and treated. Badly soaked upholstery or carpets may need replacing.

Do you work on taxis and fleet vehicles?

Yes. Ask about arrangements for taxi, private hire and fleet vehicles.

Can the work be done at my location?

It depends on the team and the job. Ask when you call whether they can attend or whether the vehicle needs to be brought in.
