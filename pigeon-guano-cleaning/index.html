Pigeon guano clean up in Birmingham

Safe removal and disinfection of pigeon droppings from lofts, roof spaces, balconies, ledges and commercial buildings.

Call 0121 000 0000 Request a free quote

Built-up pigeon droppings are more than unsightly. Dry guano can release dust containing bacteria and fungal spores, it corrodes stonework and metal, and it blocks gutters and drains. The mess is hazardous to clean, and sweeping it dry makes the dust airborne.

The specialist teams we connect you with wear respiratory protection, remove the droppings safely, disinfect the area and dispose of waste lawfully. They can also tell you where birds are getting in, so that proofing can be arranged.

What is included

  • Safe wet removal of droppings and nesting material
  • Disinfection and sanitising of the affected area
  • Removal and disposal of contaminated insulation where needed
  • Cleaning of ledges, balconies, rooflines and gutters
  • Licensed disposal of waste

Where we are called

  • Loft and roof spaces where birds have been roosting
  • Balconies, window ledges and fire escapes
  • Commercial buildings, car parks and warehouses
  • Empty or neglected properties

Clean-up removes the mess but does not stop birds returning. For lasting results you will need bird-proofing, such as netting or spikes, carried out by a pest control or proofing specialist.

Pigeon Guano Cleaning FAQs

Are pigeon droppings dangerous?

They can be. Dried droppings can carry bacteria and fungal spores that cause respiratory illness, which is why they should never be swept dry or cleaned without protection.

Can you stop the pigeons coming back?

The clean-up removes the contamination. Proofing to stop birds returning is a separate job, which the team can advise on.

Do you clean commercial buildings?

Yes. The teams work on lofts, rooflines, ledges and commercial premises.
