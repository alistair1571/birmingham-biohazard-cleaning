Needle sweeps and sharps clean up in Birmingham

Safe searching, collection and disposal of discarded needles and sharps from homes, gardens and commercial or communal areas.

Call 0121 000 0000 Request a free quote

Discarded needles and syringes are a serious hazard. A needlestick injury can put someone at risk of blood-borne infections, and children, pets and workers are all vulnerable. Finding sharps on your property is worrying, and you should never pick them up with your hands.

The teams we connect you with are trained to search for, collect and dispose of sharps safely. They carry out systematic sweeps of the area, use the correct containers and tools, and dispose of everything through the proper channels.

What is included

  • Systematic sweep of gardens, car parks, stairwells, bin stores and communal spaces
  • Safe collection into approved sharps containers
  • Cleaning and disinfection of any contaminated surfaces
  • Licensed disposal of sharps waste
  • A record of the sweep for managing agents and landlords

Where we are called

  • Rented homes where a tenant has left sharps behind
  • Gardens and outbuildings
  • Flats, stairwells and communal areas
  • Car parks, garages and commercial premises
  • Empty properties that have been entered or squatted

Never pick up a needle with bare hands. If you have been pricked by a needle, encourage the wound to bleed, wash it with soap and running water and get urgent medical advice from A&E or NHS 111. Sharps in public places can also be reported to your local council.

Needle Sweep Service FAQs

Can I put a needle in the bin?

No. Needles should not go in household rubbish or recycling. They need to go into a proper sharps container and be disposed of correctly.

Do you carry out regular sweeps for managing agents?

Where there is a recurring problem, a regular sweep can be arranged. Ask for a quote based on the site.

What if a child or pet has been in contact with a needle?

Seek urgent medical advice from A&E or NHS 111 for a person. For a pet, speak to a vet straight away.
