Human waste clean up in Birmingham

Safe, discreet removal and disinfection of faeces, urine and vomit, with odour treatment and licensed waste disposal.

Call 0121 000 0000 Request a free quote

Human waste carries bacteria and viruses that can make people seriously ill, and it soaks quickly into carpets, mattresses and floors. It is not something to tackle with rubber gloves and a mop.

The specialist teams we connect you with remove the waste, disinfect to a professional standard and treat the odour at its source. They deal with these situations regularly and treat everyone with dignity and without judgement.

What is included

  • Removal of faeces, urine, vomit and soiled materials
  • Disinfection of floors, walls and furniture
  • Removal and disposal of soaked carpets, mattresses and upholstery
  • Deodorising treatment to neutralise odours
  • Licensed disposal of contaminated waste

When this happens

  • A property has been left in a neglected or unsanitary state
  • A vulnerable person or relative has been unable to manage personal care at home
  • A tenant has left a property contaminated
  • A property has been squatted or used while empty
  • A blocked or failed toilet has overflowed

Human Waste Cleaning FAQs

Is human waste dangerous?

Yes. It can contain bacteria such as E. coli and salmonella and viruses that cause stomach illness. Proper protective equipment and disinfectants are needed.

Can carpets and mattresses be saved?

Often not. Waste soaks deeply into porous materials, and removal is usually the safest option. The team will tell you what can and cannot be treated.

Do you work for landlords and letting agents?

Yes. Landlords, agents and housing associations often need a rented property returned to a clean, lettable state, and the teams are used to working with them.
