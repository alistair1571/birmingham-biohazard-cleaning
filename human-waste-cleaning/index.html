Human waste cleaning in Birmingham

Safe, discreet removal and disinfection of faeces, urine and vomit, with odour treatment and lawful disposal. Anywhere in Birmingham and the West Midlands, at any hour.

Call 0121 000 0000 Request a free quote

It's more common than anyone talks about, and there's nothing to be embarrassed about. You won't be the first to call us about it.

Human waste in a home or building isn't something you can wipe up and forget. It carries harmful bacteria and viruses, it soaks into carpets, mattresses and floors, and the smell tends to get worse, not better. It's also one of the hardest things to ask for help with, because it's so personal.

The reasons vary. A relative who can no longer manage personal care. A tenant who has left a property in a state. Squatters, or someone sleeping rough in a stairwell, garage or empty unit. A toilet that has failed and overflowed. An illness that has swept through a household or a workplace. Whatever the cause, you need it dealt with safely and quickly, without a fuss.

Get in touch and we'll put the right team on it, and explain what the job involves and what it will cost before anyone starts. No lectures, and no questions about how it happened.

What's included

  • Removal of waste and soiled materials
  • Cleaning and disinfection of floors, walls, furniture and fittings, including the gaps and edges where liquid runs
  • Treatment or removal of soaked carpets, mattresses and upholstery, with honest advice on which is which
  • Checks for soak-through to underlay and floorboards
  • Odour treatment at the source, not just a cover-up
  • Safe handling of any needles or sharps found along the way
  • Contaminated waste bagged, collected and disposed of lawfully, with records

When this happens

  • A neglected or unsanitary home
  • A relative or someone you care for can no longer manage personal care
  • A tenant has left a property contaminated: Landlords and letting agents
  • A property has been squatted or used while empty, or someone has been sleeping rough in a garage, stairwell or commercial unit
  • A toilet has blocked or failed and overflowed: see sewage clean-up
  • Illness has spread through a home, care setting or business, and vomit or diarrhoea has soiled floors and furnishings

What you can safely do right now

While you wait for help:

  • Keep people, children and pets away from the area, and keep the door to the room shut. If you can, open a window.
  • Don't walk through it and carry it around the house.
  • Wear disposable gloves if you have to go near it, and wash your hands with soap and water afterwards. Alcohol gel doesn't kill norovirus, the stomach bug that spreads through faeces.
  • Wash soiled clothes and bedding separately from other laundry, at 60°C.
  • Don't vacuum it, and don't put contaminated items in an ordinary bin.

For a small, one-off accident on a hard floor, careful household hygiene may be all you need. It's more than that, and worth calling us, when:

  • waste has soaked into carpet, a mattress or floorboards
  • there's a large amount, or it has been there for days or longer
  • more than one room is affected
  • someone vulnerable lives in the home
  • you're a landlord, agent or business, and need it done properly and documented

Why wiping it up isn't enough

Human waste carries bacteria such as E. coli and salmonella, and viruses such as norovirus, which spreads easily on contaminated surfaces. Children, older people and anyone with a weakened immune system are at greater risk of becoming seriously ill.

The surface is rarely the whole problem. Liquid soaks down through carpet into underlay and floorboards, and urine in particular can leave a smell that comes back whenever the room gets warm or damp. Porous materials that have been soaked generally can't be made safe, which is why carpets, mattresses and sofas often have to go. If waste is left for a long time, it can soak deeper and raise the cost.

If you're caring for someone

If you've come to this page because someone you look after can't manage, please know it isn't your fault, and it isn't theirs. Incontinence and illness are medical issues, not failings. The team deals with these situations matter-of-factly and in private, so nobody feels ashamed.

Their GP can advise on continence support, and the council's adult social care team can assess their care needs. We can clean up, but getting the right support in place is what stops it happening again.

For landlords, agents, housing teams and businesses

Empty or squatted properties and rough sleeping. Waste often comes with other hazards, including needles hidden in bedding or bags. Tell us if you've seen any, and see our needle sweep service. Take dated photos before the clean. A record of the work afterwards helps with insurers, council teams and deposit discussions.

Left-behind belongings. Landlords usually can't get rid of a tenant's belongings without going through the proper process, so take advice first. That's general guidance, not a legal opinion.

Businesses. After an illness outbreak or an incident at your premises, you'll want to reopen safely and with a record of what was done. Tell us when you need to be back in business, and we'll plan the work around that where possible.

What affects the price

No two clean-ups are the same, so each is priced on its own merits. What drives the cost:

  • What it is and how much of it
  • What it has soaked into. A hard floor is much quicker than carpet, a mattress or floorboards
  • How long it has been there
  • How many rooms are affected
  • How much has to come out, such as carpet, underlay or furniture
  • Access and urgency, including out-of-hours calls

The quote is free, and you'll know the price before any work starts.

Human Waste Cleaning FAQs

Is human waste dangerous?

Yes. It can carry harmful bacteria and viruses, and the risk is higher for children, older people and anyone with a weakened immune system. Specialist protective equipment and proper disinfection are needed to make an area safe again.

Can I clean it myself?

A small, one-off accident on a hard floor can often be handled with careful hygiene, as set out above. If waste has soaked into carpet, a mattress or floorboards, or it's more than one room, we'd advise a specialist. It's hard to tell by eye how far it has gone.

Can carpets and mattresses be saved?

Often not, because they're porous and liquid soaks through. Removal is usually the safest option. The team will advise on what can be treated and what has to go.

Do we need to leave the property?

You don't have to leave the whole property, but keep the affected rooms out of use, and keep children, older people and anyone vulnerable away until the work is complete and signed off.

How quickly can someone come?

The phone line is open around the clock. Tell us it's urgent and we'll tell you the earliest the team can attend.

What if it has been there for days or weeks?

It can still be cleaned. It's likely to have soaked deeper, so more may need to be removed, and your quote will reflect that.

Do you work with landlords and letting agents?

Yes, including housing associations returning a rented property to a lettable state.

Will it be embarrassing?

It doesn't need to be. Nobody will ask how it happened or pass comment. The job is simply to make the space safe again, and we keep things private.

Where does the waste go?

Contaminated waste is taken away by a registered carrier for lawful disposal, and you receive the records.
