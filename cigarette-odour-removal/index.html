Cigarette odour removal in Birmingham

Deep cleaning and odour treatment to remove the smell of cigarette smoke from homes, rental properties and commercial premises.

Call 0121 000 0000 Request a free quote

Years of smoking leave a sticky film of nicotine and tar on walls, ceilings, windows and furnishings. That residue holds the smell, so repainting or an air freshener alone rarely fixes it, and it can stain paintwork yellow.

The teams we connect you with clean the residue from every surface, treat carpets and soft furnishings, and use odour-neutralising treatment to remove the smell at its source. They also advise on what is worth replacing and on sealing surfaces before redecoration.

What is included

  • Cleaning of walls, ceilings, windows, doors and fixtures
  • Deep cleaning of carpets, curtains and soft furnishings
  • Odour-neutralising treatment of the property
  • Advice on sealing surfaces before repainting
  • A final check for remaining odour

When you might need it

  • A landlord getting a property ready to re-let after a smoking tenant
  • Selling or buying a home with a smoke smell
  • Moving into a second-hand property or flat
  • Offices, shops, pubs and other commercial premises
  • Resolving disputes about smoking in a rental

Cigarette Odour Removal FAQs

Can the smell be removed completely?

Usually it can be removed or greatly reduced. After many years of heavy smoking, carpets, curtains and some paintwork may need replacing, and the team will tell you what is realistic.

Will repainting fix it?

Not on its own. Nicotine residue can bleed through new paint and bring the smell back. Surfaces need cleaning and sealing first.

Can landlords use this to get a property re-let?

Yes. It is a common job for landlords and agents. Ask for an itemised quote and photographs for your records.
