Privacy policy

Last updated 8 October 2026

Who we are

Birmingham Biohazard Cleaning is a referral service that connects customers with independent specialist cleaning teams. [Add registered business name and address here.] You can contact us at info@birminghambiohazardcleaning.co.uk.

What information we collect

When you send an enquiry or call us, we collect the details you give us, such as your name, phone number, email address, property postcode and a description of the job.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and arrange a quote
  • To pass your enquiry to a specialist cleaning team who can help
  • To keep records of enquiries and improve our service

Who we share it with

We share your details only with the specialist cleaning team that will contact you about your enquiry, and with service providers who help us run this website, such as our form and hosting providers. We do not sell your data.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry and for a reasonable period afterwards for our records, then delete them.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, and to object to how we use it. Contact us using the email above. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies and third parties

This website does not use advertising cookies. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when the page loads.
