After death cleaning in Birmingham

Gentle, discreet clean-up and odour removal after a death at home, so that families and landlords do not have to face it alone.

Call 0121 000 0000 Request a free quote

When someone dies at home, and especially when they were not found for days or weeks, the property can be left contaminated by bodily fluids and a strong, lingering odour. Dealing with that while grieving is more than anyone should have to do.

The specialist teams we work with are experienced in these situations. They approach every property with respect and without judgement, remove contaminated materials, disinfect thoroughly and tackle odour at the source, so the home can be returned to a safe, habitable condition.

What is included

  • Removal of contaminated bedding, carpets, mattresses and furniture
  • Treatment of floors, subfloors and porous surfaces where fluids have soaked in
  • Full disinfection of the affected rooms
  • Professional odour neutralisation, not just air fresheners
  • Careful handling of personal belongings on request
  • Safe, licensed disposal of all contaminated waste

Situations we help with

  • Unattended or undiscovered deaths
  • Deaths where bodily fluids have contaminated flooring or furnishings
  • Properties that need to be cleared and cleaned ahead of probate or sale
  • Landlords dealing with a death in a rented home
  • Housing associations and managing agents who need a fast, discreet response

Cleaning can only begin once the police, coroner and funeral director have finished and the property has been released. If you are unsure of the position, ask the officer or funeral director dealing with the case. If you are struggling with what has happened, the Samaritans are available any time on 116 123.

After Death Cleaning FAQs

How soon after a death can the clean-up start?

Once the deceased has been removed and the property has been released by the police or coroner, if they were involved. Acting quickly helps, because odours and contamination can spread into floors and furnishings.

Will the smell definitely go?

In most cases, yes, provided the contaminated materials are removed and the source is treated. Where fluids have soaked into floorboards or subfloors, extra treatment may be needed, and the team will explain this in the quote.

Who pays for after death cleaning?

Usually the homeowner, the estate or the landlord, depending on the situation. Some home insurance policies contribute, so it is worth checking. Ask for an itemised quote to support any claim.
