Hoarder clean up in Birmingham

Respectful, judgement-free hoarding clearance and deep cleaning for homes across Birmingham and the West Midlands.

Call 0121 000 0000 Request a free quote

Hoarding is far more common than people think, and it is rarely about laziness. Many people find themselves overwhelmed by belongings, and a home can reach a point where it is unsafe, unhygienic or impossible to live in comfortably. Asking for help is a big step, and it deserves a kind and practical response.

The teams we connect you with have seen it all and judge nothing. They work with you to agree what stays and what goes, sort and clear the property carefully, remove waste lawfully and deep clean the space afterwards. Where it is needed, they can work in stages at a pace that suits you.

What is included

  • A discreet assessment and clear quote before any work starts
  • Sorting and clearing, with items of value or sentimental importance set aside
  • Removal of rubbish, food waste, soiled items and bulky furniture
  • Treatment of pest droppings, mould or biohazards found underneath
  • Deep clean and disinfection of every affected room
  • Licensed waste removal and disposal

Common situations

  • A family member is worried about a relative's home
  • A tenant's property is in an unsafe or unhygienic state and the landlord needs it resolved
  • A housing association or council has concerns about fire risk or access
  • A home needs clearing ahead of a repair, sale or tenancy ending
  • Someone has decided they are ready for help and wants it done sensitively

Hoarding is recognised as a mental health condition. If you are supporting someone, a kind, patient approach works far better than pressure. Their GP can offer support and referrals, and your local fire service may be able to advise on home fire safety.

Hoarders Clean Up FAQs

Will you judge the state of my home?

No. The teams work with homes in every condition, every week. The aim is to help you, not to comment on how things got there.

Can the work be done in stages?

Often, yes. For some people a gradual approach is easier, and the team can agree a plan that works for you or your family.

What happens to my belongings?

You decide what is kept. Items of sentimental or financial value are set aside, and what remains is removed and disposed of lawfully.

How much does hoarder clean up cost?

It depends on the volume of belongings, the level of contamination, access and how many people are needed. A free quote is the best starting point, and photographs of the property help us give a realistic estimate.
