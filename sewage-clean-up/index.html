Sewage clean up in Birmingham

Fast, thorough clean-up and disinfection after sewage leaks, backflow and overflows in homes and commercial premises.

Call 0121 000 0000 Request a free quote

Sewage contamination is classed as the most hazardous kind of water damage. It carries harmful bacteria and viruses, spreads quickly into floors and walls and leaves a persistent smell. The sooner it is dealt with, the less damage it causes.

The teams we connect you with remove the contaminated water and solids, disinfect every affected surface, dry the area and dispose of contaminated materials lawfully. They can also advise on what to do about the cause.

What is included

  • Extraction of contaminated water and removal of solids
  • Removal of affected carpets, underlay and damaged materials
  • Cleaning and disinfection of walls, floors and fittings
  • Drying and dehumidifying of the affected area
  • Odour treatment
  • Licensed disposal of contaminated waste

Typical causes

  • Blocked or collapsed drains and sewers
  • Toilets, washing machines or waste pipes overflowing
  • Sewage backing up through floor drains, sinks or showers
  • Flooding that has mixed with foul water
  • Failed pumps or macerators

Keep children and pets away from the affected area and avoid touching contaminated water. If the sewage is coming from a public sewer, contact your water company (in most of the Birmingham area this is Severn Trent). If it is from private drains or plumbing, you may need a plumber or drainage specialist as well.

Sewage Clean Up FAQs

Is sewage damage covered by insurance?

Often, yes, particularly under buildings insurance where the cause is covered. Check your policy and report the incident as soon as you can.

How quickly do I need to act?

As quickly as possible. Moisture and bacteria spread fast, and delays increase damage, smell and cost.

Can you help commercial premises?

Yes. The teams work in homes, shops, offices and other commercial buildings.
