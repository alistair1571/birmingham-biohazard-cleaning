Found a Cannabis Farm in Your Rental? What to Do Next

Published 8 October 2026

1. Do not enter until you know it is safe

Cannabis farms commonly involve illegal electrical connections, and the property may have live wiring, high humidity and mould. If you suspect a grow, do not go in. Contact the police.

2. Let the police finish

The police will secure and investigate the property. You will not be able to start work until they have released it, so ask the officer in charge when access is permitted.

3. Tell your insurer and your agent

Report the incident to your insurer promptly and read your policy. Cover for damage caused by illegal activity varies. If an agent manages the property, keep them informed.

4. Get the electrics checked

Do not restore power until a qualified electrician has inspected the installation. Bypassed meters and tampered wiring are a fire and shock risk.

5. Arrange specialist clean-up

Growing equipment, soil, chemicals and plant waste must be removed and disposed of through a registered waste carrier. Mould and damp will also need treating before any redecorating.

6. Keep records

Photograph everything and keep itemised quotes and invoices. They support insurance claims and any action against the tenant.

This guide is general information, not legal advice. Speak to a solicitor about your rights as a landlord.
