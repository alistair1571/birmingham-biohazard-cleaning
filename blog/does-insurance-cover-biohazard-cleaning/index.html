Does Home Insurance Cover Biohazard Cleaning?

Published 8 October 2026

It depends on the policy

There is no single rule. Some buildings and contents policies cover cleaning after specific events, such as escape of water or sewage, a death at the property or malicious damage. Others exclude it or limit the amount. Landlord policies can differ again.

What to check

  • Whether the cause of the problem is a covered event
  • Whether cleaning and decontamination costs are included
  • Any excess you would need to pay
  • Whether the policy excludes illegal activity or neglect
  • Any time limits for reporting a claim

How to improve your chances

Contact your insurer as soon as possible, before you throw anything away or start work. Take photographs, keep receipts and ask your cleaning provider for an itemised quote. Some insurers have their own approved contractors, so ask before you book.

If the claim is declined

Ask for the reasons in writing. You can complain to the insurer and, if you are unhappy with the outcome, to the Financial Ombudsman Service.

This is general information, not insurance or legal advice. Always check your own policy documents.
