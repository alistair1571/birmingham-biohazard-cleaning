Fire damage cleaning in Birmingham

Thorough soot removal, cleaning and deodorising after a fire, so a damaged property can begin to be restored.

Call 0121 000 0000 Request a free quote

Even a small fire leaves a mess far beyond the room where it started. Soot settles on every surface, smoke travels through the building, and the water used to put the fire out causes damage of its own. Soot is also acidic, so the longer it sits, the harder it is to clean and the more it can stain and corrode.

The specialist teams we connect you with clean fire-affected properties methodically, from ceilings down to floors. They remove soot and debris, clean and deodorise surfaces and contents where possible, and dispose of items that cannot be saved, so the property is ready for repairs and redecoration.

What is included

  • Assessment of the damage and a clear, itemised quote
  • Removal of loose debris, fire-damaged and water-soaked materials
  • Soot removal from walls, ceilings, floors, fixtures and fittings
  • Cleaning of belongings and furnishings that can be saved
  • Deodorising to deal with lingering smoke odours
  • Safe disposal of waste, and photographic records for your insurer

When you might need it

  • After a kitchen fire, even a small one
  • After an electrical or appliance fire
  • After a house, flat or commercial building fire
  • Where a neighbour's fire has left smoke and soot in your property
  • Landlords and managing agents dealing with a fire in a let property

Do not re-enter a property after a fire until the fire service says it is safe. Structural and electrical checks may be needed before work begins, and you should contact your insurer as early as you can.

Fire Damage Cleaning FAQs

Is fire damage cleaning covered by insurance?

Often, yes, under buildings and contents insurance. Contact your insurer straight away, and ask for an itemised quote and photographs to support the claim.

How soon should cleaning start?

As soon as the property is safe to enter. Soot and smoke residue become harder to remove over time and can stain and corrode surfaces.

Can belongings be saved?

Many hard items can be cleaned. Porous and heavily smoke-affected items such as soft furnishings are harder to restore, and the team will tell you what is realistic.
