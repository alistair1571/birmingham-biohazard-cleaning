Why use a specialist

Specialist team or DIY? It is not close

Biohazards are a health risk. Here is how a specialist team compares with doing it yourself or using a general cleaner.

A specialist biohazard team

  • Proper protective equipmentSuits, respirators and gloves rated for biological hazards
  • Professional disinfectionHospital-grade products used correctly
  • Lawful waste disposalContaminated waste carried by a registered carrier
  • Odour removed at the sourceContaminated materials removed, not masked
  • Discreet and respectfulUsed to sensitive situations, with no judgement

DIY or a general cleaner

  • Basic or no protectionExposure to blood-borne and other infections
  • Household productsOften not enough to disinfect properly
  • Waste left to youContaminated waste cannot go in the household bin
  • Smells that returnFluids that have soaked in are missed
  • Distressing for youNot something you should have to face yourself

FAQ

Your questions answered

How quickly can someone attend?

Our phone line is open 24 hours a day. Response times depend on the job and on team availability, so call and describe the situation for the quickest answer.

Is the service discreet?

Yes. These jobs are often sensitive, and the teams we connect you with are asked to work quietly and keep every detail private.

Will I be judged?

No. The teams see homes in every condition and treat every customer with kindness and respect.

Do you handle waste disposal?

Yes. Contaminated and hazardous waste has to be carried and disposed of by a registered waste carrier. The teams we work with take care of this as part of the job.

Do you work with landlords, agents and housing associations?

Yes. We work with homeowners, families, landlords, letting agents, housing associations and commercial property managers.

How much will it cost?

Every job is different. Cost depends on the type of contamination, the size of the area and how much has to be removed. You will get a free, no-obligation quote, and photographs help us estimate quickly.

Can I get a quote without a visit?

Often, yes. Photographs and a description are enough for many jobs. For larger or more complex properties the team may visit to assess first.

Is biohazard cleaning covered by insurance?

Sometimes. Cover depends on the policy and the cause. Check your policy and ask your insurer, and request an itemised quote to support any claim.

Do you only do biohazard cleaning?

No. As well as biohazard clean-ups, we can connect you with teams for fire and smoke damage, flood and water damage, and odour removal.

Which areas do you cover?

We cover Birmingham and the surrounding West Midlands, including Wolverhampton, Coventry, Walsall, Solihull, Dudley, West Bromwich, Sutton Coldfield, Smethwick, Halesowen, Stourbridge, Lichfield and Tamworth.
