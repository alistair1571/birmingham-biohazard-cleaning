Flood damage clean up in Birmingham

Fast water extraction, drying and clean-up after floods, burst pipes and leaks, for homes and commercial properties.

Call 0121 000 0000 Request a free quote

Water damage gets worse by the hour. It soaks into plaster, floors and furnishings, and mould can begin to grow within a couple of days. The sooner the water is removed and the property is dried, the less damage you will have to repair.

The specialist teams we connect you with extract standing water, remove materials that are beyond saving, dry the property with professional equipment and treat it to prevent mould. Where floodwater is contaminated, they disinfect as well.

What is included

  • Extraction of standing water
  • Removal of soaked carpets, underlay and damaged materials
  • Professional drying and dehumidifying of floors and walls
  • Antimicrobial and mould-prevention treatment
  • Disinfection where floodwater is contaminated
  • Records and photographs to support insurance claims

Typical causes

  • Burst or leaking pipes and tanks
  • Washing machine, dishwasher or boiler leaks
  • Overflowing baths, sinks and toilets
  • Heavy rain, storms and surface water flooding
  • Roof and gutter failures

If it is safe to do so, turn off the water at the stopcock and the electricity at the consumer unit, and avoid using electrical appliances in wet areas. Floodwater can be contaminated, so avoid contact where you can. The GOV.UK flood warning service shows current flood alerts for your area.

Flood Damage Clean Up FAQs

How long does drying take?

It depends on the extent of the water and the materials affected. Drying often takes several days, and the team will monitor moisture levels so that it is done properly.

Is flood damage covered by insurance?

Often, yes, particularly for burst pipes and escape of water. Contact your insurer as soon as possible and keep photographs and receipts.

Will mould develop?

It can if the property is not dried quickly and thoroughly. Prompt extraction, drying and antimicrobial treatment greatly reduce the risk.
