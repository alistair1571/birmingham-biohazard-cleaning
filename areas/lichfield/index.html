Biohazard cleaning in Lichfield

Specialist clean-up for houses, flats, listed buildings and outbuildings across Lichfield and the surrounding villages, including Burntwood, Whittington, Shenstone, Fradley, Alrewas and Streethay. Whether it follows a death, rodent damage, hoarding, a sewage problem or a long-empty home.

Lichfield feels close to Birmingham, but it is in Staffordshire, and that changes who you contact. The first section sets it out.

Lichfield is a small cathedral city of about 35,000 people, in a district of about 106,000, and it sits roughly 14 miles north of Birmingham. Commuters use the Cross-City Line, the A38 and the M6 Toll, and many of the people who ring us from other parts of the country are relatives of someone who lives here. But officially it belongs to Staffordshire, not the West Midlands, so the councils, the police and the coroner all differ from those on our Black Country pages.

That is the first thing to understand, because the next question is always "who do I tell?" This page answers that, and then deals with what is particular to Lichfield: a historic centre with over 230 listed buildings and narrow streets, two pools in the middle of the city, a calendar of events that closes roads, a lot of owners who have lived in the same house for decades, and relatives who live a long way away. Ring or use the form and we source a team for the property, with a line-by-line price that you approve before anything begins. Arranging it is our role; the crew who attend do the cleaning.

Staffordshire has two tiers of local government, so the same address deals with a district council for some things and the county council for others. If you are worried about an older neighbour or relative, the county council's adult social care team is usually the right route; for rubbish or a neglected property, start with the district.

In an emergency dial 999. To ask police when a property will be released after a death or an incident, ring 101. Nothing can be cleaned until it is.

Rodents with no council treatment: what that means

Because the district council doesn't treat rats and mice in homes, the process is simpler but you do more of it yourself.

  1. Book an accredited pest controllerand ask for something in writing that records what they found and what they did.
  2. Hold off cleaning until treatment is finished.Cleaning first only disturbs the animals and the droppings.
  3. Leave droppings and loft insulation untouchedmeanwhile, since dry sweeping or vacuuming throws harmful dust into the air.
  4. Ring usso the clean-up can be planned for the day treatment is complete. See after rodent clean up.
  5. Tenantsshould put it to the landlord or agent in writing and keep a copy of the message.

A historic centre: narrow streets, listed buildings and event days

Roger de Clinton laid out the city's ladder-plan street pattern in the 12th century, and the centre still has over 230 listed buildings, many Georgian. For a cleaning team, that has three practical effects.

  • Access. Narrow streets, restricted parking and the Cathedral Close make it harder to bring a van close. Tell us where the nearest place to stop is, and how far the carry is.
  • Event days. The Lichfield Festival each July, the Greenhill Bower on the Spring Bank Holiday and the triennial Lichfield Mysteries bring crowds and closures. Mention the date when you book.
  • Careful methods. Old plaster, timber floors and traditional finishes don't always cope with aggressive chemicals or heavy steam. Tell us if the building is listed or in a conservation area, and ask the council whether consent is needed for any work beyond cleaning.

Fire, smoke and flood in an old building call for gentler methods and a longer drying time; see smoke damage cleaning and flood damage clean up.

The pools, the canals and where the water goes

Minster Pool and Stowe Pool lie in the middle of the city, and Stowe Pool is a Site of Special Scientific Interest. The Coventry Canal runs through Streethay, and parts of the old Lichfield Canal survive. All of that means waste water matters here.

  • Contaminated water must not go down a surface drain or into a watercourse. Ask any contractor how they dispose of wash water and waste, and for the transfer paperwork.
  • Waterside gardens are a common place for rats.
  • After a flood or a sewage backup, the water carries contamination. See sewage clean-up.

Living far away: relatives and executors

Lichfield has an older population, and many calls come from people who live elsewhere. The Cross-City Line runs to Birmingham New Street, and Lichfield Trent Valley is on the West Coast Main Line, with Euston as little as an hour and ten minutes away. That is close enough to visit, but not to be there every day. If you're managing from a distance:

  1. Confirm who has authoritybefore anything is cleared. See who cleans up after a death in a property.
  2. Use photographs first.A short video call or a set of dated photos is often enough for a first quote.
  3. Leave the keys with someone you trust,such as a neighbour, solicitor or agent.
  4. List what must not be lostbefore the team arrives: papers, jewellery, medication, photographs.
  5. Ask for a written report and dated before-and-after photosto be sent to you.

For a death at home, see after death cleaning. For a house full of belongings, hoarders clean up and who pays for a hoarder clean up explain the staged approach.

Villages and the rural edge

Beyond the city, Whittington, Shenstone, Fradley and Alrewas have older cottages, farm buildings and outlying homes, some of which use private drainage rather than a public sewer. Our Solihull page explains what to do if a septic tank or private drain fails. Garages, barns and sheds attract rodents, and bats and nesting birds are protected, so get advice before blocking a roof space.

What changes the price

Price depends on how much has to go, what it is, how long it has sat, the distance to the van and how soon you need it. Listed or hard-to-reach buildings can need more time. A first figure normally comes from photos, and any extra work is re-quoted before it happens.

Lichfield biohazard cleaning questions

Do you cover Lichfield and Burntwood?

Yes, along with Whittington, Shenstone, Fradley, Alrewas and Streethay, plus nearby Brownhills and Sutton Coldfield.

Which police force covers Lichfield?

Staffordshire Police, since the city is in Staffordshire.

Which coroner deals with a death in Lichfield?

The Staffordshire and Stoke-on-Trent Coroners Service. The funeral director or the officer dealing with the death can confirm.

Does the council deal with rats?

Lichfield District Council says it doesn't treat rats and mice in homes and gardens, so you need an accredited private firm. We clean up afterwards.

Can you clean a listed building?

Yes, if you tell us in advance so suitable methods are used.

I live far away. Can you manage without me?

Yes. A neighbour, solicitor or agent can hold the keys, and photographs and a report follow by email.
