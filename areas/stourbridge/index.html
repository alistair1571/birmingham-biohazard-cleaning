Biohazard cleaning in Stourbridge, DY8 and DY9

Stourbridge is a market town in the Metropolitan Borough of Dudley, on the River Stour, and the western gateway to the Black Country. Its postcodes are DY8 and DY9, and it adjoins Lye, Wollaston, Amblecote, Wordsley, Pedmore and Oldswinford. Callers sometimes describe an address as Kingswinford or Halesowen; both are covered.

The specialist teams cover the entire town and its surroundings. Give the postcode when you call, describe what has happened and ask for a written quote.

Glass, canals and Victorian streets

Stourbridge is renowned for glass-making, and the Stourbridge Canal and its arm to Fens Pool are among the town's most distinctive features. The town centre has a large stock of Victorian and Edwardian buildings, including flats above shops, and older terraced streets towards Amblecote and Wollaston.

Flats above shops often share entrances and stairs, so access and discretion matter. When you book, say if the property is above a business so the work can be planned around opening hours.

Suburbs, villages and elderly owners

Pedmore, Oldswinford and Norton are leafy residential areas with larger houses, many lived in by the same owners for decades. Lye and Wollaston are more densely built, with terraces and a good number of private lets. In every case the pattern is similar: a death, illness or house sale reveals a property that needs specialist cleaning.

See our pages on after-death cleaning, hoarder clean-up and landlords and agents for the usual jobs, and the first-hour advice for what to do before anyone arrives.

Stations, flooding and local contacts

Stourbridge Town station is the end of the Stourbridge Shuttle, one of the shortest branch lines in Britain, and Stourbridge Junction is on the main line to Birmingham and Worcester. The River Stour and its tributaries are prone to flooding in heavy rain. If water or sewage has entered a property, see our flood damage and sewage clean-up pages.

Dudley Council is the local authority, and West Midlands Police and West Midlands Fire Service cover the town.

Quotes, insurance and credentials

Before you commit, ask for the waste carrier registration number, proof of public liability insurance and, for the larger jobs, a written risk assessment. These are basic requirements, and a team that cannot provide them should not be doing the work. Check the registration on the Environment Agency's public register.

Be cautious about claims such as 'fully accredited' or 'certified' unless the team can say by whom and show a certificate. Ask what training the staff have for blood-borne hazards, sewage and asbestos awareness, and what protective equipment they use.

A common situation in Stourbridge

In a town with a lot of older owner-occupied housing, a typical request follows a bereavement. A relative arrives to find the house unlived-in for a while, perhaps with a bad smell and signs that the person had been unwell for some time. They want to know what is safe to touch, who to call and how much it will cost.

The answer is to leave the area affected, ventilate if possible, and call for an assessment. The team will explain which materials can be cleaned and which must go, and they can arrange the clear-out and odour treatment so that the house can be sold or let. A written quote with photographs helps the family, the executors and the solicitor keep track.

Who to contact in Stourbridge

For an emergency, always call 999. For a non-emergency police matter, the force covering Stourbridge is West Midlands Police, and the fire and rescue service is West Midlands Fire Service. The local authority is Dudley Council, which handles environmental health, empty and neglected properties, and waste. The area uses the postcodes DY8 and DY9, and nearby places include Wollaston, Amblecote, Lye, Pedmore, Norton, Wordsley.

If a sudden death, crime or fire has happened, no cleaning can start until the emergency services have released the property. Once they have, call for an assessment.
