Biohazard cleaning in Aldridge

Specialist clean-up for family homes, rentals and business units in Aldridge, Leighswood, Walsall Wood and the WS9 postcode. After a death, hoarding, a sewage backup, needles or pigeon droppings, the work is arranged around how the property is owned and who has to approve it.

Aldridge sits in an odd spot on the map. It has a Walsall postcode and Walsall Council, it borders Streetly and Sutton Coldfield, which are covered by other authorities, and it sits a few minutes from Lichfield district. People searching from here often type in the name of a bigger town nearby, then find that the advice they've been given doesn't apply to their address.

This page is written for Aldridge in particular. It covers the two kinds of call that come from the area, a family home that has become unmanageable or been left after a death, and a business unit, shop or workshop with a contamination problem. It also covers the local detail that catches people out: which council, which coroner, and what the council will and won't do. Call or message us and a specialist team can be lined up, with the cost set out line by line in writing before anyone starts. We coordinate; the attending team does the cleaning.

What's included

  • A first call that works out what has happened, who owns the property and what should happen before anyone goes in
  • A look at the property from photographs or a visit, then a written quote broken down by task
  • Taking out what's contaminated beyond saving, then cleaning and disinfecting every affected surface

Family homes that stay in the family

A lot of Aldridge was built from the 1930s onwards, and many of those houses have stayed with one family through two or three generations. That has a few consequences when something goes wrong.

  • Contents build up over decades. A loft, a garage and a back bedroom can hold years of belongings. When a house is left after a death, a hospital stay or a fall, the first look can be a shock.
  • Hoarding is often discovered, not planned. Families commonly find it when they get access for the first time in years. Approach it calmly: agree what matters and what doesn't before anything is touched, and don't clear anything without the owner's agreement unless you have the legal authority to. Our hoarders clean up page explains how a staged clean works, and the money side is in our blog post, who pays for a hoarder clean up.
  • A death at home. Nothing can be cleaned until the property is released by the police or coroner. After death cleaning covers the sequence, and who cleans up after a death in a property explains who is responsible for each kind of home.
  • Executors who live elsewhere. Many do. A remote survey from photographs, a written quote and a photo record of each stage can usually take the place of being there, and a neighbour or relative can hold the keys.
  • Cleaning and clearance are different jobs. One removes contamination; the other empties the house. If you need both, have them priced as two lines so a solicitor can read the invoice easily.

Leighswood and the business units

Aldridge also has trading estates and industrial units, and Leighswood is the area people tend to mean. The calls from there are different from the ones from a family home.

  • Needles and sharps. Unit yards, loading areas, bin stores and the edges of car parks sometimes have needles left in them. Leave them where they are and keep people away; the needle sweep service page describes safe handling and how a sweep is run.
  • Pigeon droppings. Large roofs, roller shutters and open-sided units are ideal roosts, and the droppings build up in eaves, gutters and loading bays. Dry droppings shouldn't be swept. Our pigeon guano cleaning page goes through the risks. A clean doesn't stop the birds returning, so ask a pest specialist about proofing.
  • Sewage and drainage failures. A blocked drain or backed-up toilet block in a unit can close a business for the day. See sewage clean-up.
  • Out-of-hours incidents. Break-ins, fire, flooding or a vacated unit left in a state often need attention before staff come back.
  • Tenants and landlords. In a rented unit, check the lease for who is responsible for what, and tell the landlord and insurer before you commit to work you may want to claim for. Our landlords and letting agents page explains who needs to agree to the work.
  • Paperwork. Businesses producing contaminated waste have obligations for how it is handled. Ask for waste carrier details and keep the records.

A quick written quote, dated before-and-after photos and the waste paperwork are what most businesses ask for. Tell us your reopening deadline when you ring and we'll say what is realistic.

Walsall, not Birmingham: who to contact

This is the part that trips people up. Aldridge is in the Metropolitan Borough of Walsall. It was part of Staffordshire until 1974. A few minutes away, Streetly and Sutton Coldfield are mostly under Birmingham City Council, and Lichfield and Burntwood fall under Lichfield District Council.

  • Council. For an address in Aldridge, the council is Walsall Council. If you are not sure, check your council tax bill.
  • Police and fire. West Midlands Police and West Midlands Fire Service cover Aldridge, but if your address is close to the Staffordshire border, check which force is involved.
  • Coroner. Walsall is covered by the Black Country Coroner Service, which serves Sandwell, Dudley, Walsall and Wolverhampton and sits at the Coroners Court in Oldbury. This is a different service from the Birmingham and Solihull coroner that covers Birmingham itself. If the death is in Aldridge, the police officer in charge or the funeral director can tell you which coroner is dealing with it.
  • Pest control. Walsall Council says it has withdrawn its pest control service and suggests using a professional company, ideally one that belongs to a trade body such as the BPCA or NPTA. Check the council's current page.
  • Rubbish on private land. The council says it can act on rubbish on private land or in gardens that attracts rats or mice or poses a public health risk, and you can report this to it. That is a route if a neighbour's property is the problem.
  • Water. Sewage from a public sewer is for the water company. A blocked private drain is normally the owner's responsibility.

Canals, quarries and open ground

Aldridge has a canal marina, a local nature reserve at Leigh's Wood, and old industrial land, including clay and brick country. Open ground and old waterways mean rats, damp and the occasional flood can all be part of the picture. If you have had water in the house, see flood damage clean up and flooded basement clean up. For rodents in a loft, garage or outbuilding, see after rodent clean up, and remember that the cleaning comes after pest control, not instead of it.

Rented homes and landlords

Aldridge has plenty of houses and flats let by owners who live elsewhere. If a tenant has left the property in a poor state, has died, or has used it for something illegal, the landlord can deal with everything remotely through an agent: photographs, an itemised quote, a documented clean and a handover. Aldridge biohazard cleaning questions

Does this cover Aldridge and WS9?

Yes, including Leighswood, Walsall Wood, Streetly, Brownhills and Sutton Coldfield. If you're unsure whether you're in our area, give us your postcode.

Can a house be cleared after a death?

Yes. Say what you'd like kept, and the team can clean and clear the rest. Quote for the two jobs separately if you need that.

Is there a railway station in Aldridge?

No. The passenger station closed in 1966 and the line now carries freight only. When booking, mention parking and access at the property.

Which council deals with a neglected property?

For an address in Aldridge, Walsall Council. Birmingham City Council covers Sutton Coldfield and most of Streetly, and Lichfield District Council covers Lichfield and Burntwood. Call 999 if someone is in immediate danger.

Does Walsall Council do pest control?

The council says it no longer provides a service and suggests a professional company. Check its website in case that has changed.

Can you clean a business unit out of hours?

Usually, at extra cost. Tell us your opening time and we'll say what is realistic.

What if I live a long way away?

Many callers do. We can work from photographs, send a record back and deal with a solicitor or managing agent for you.
