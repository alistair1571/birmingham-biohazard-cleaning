Biohazard cleaning in Tipton, DY4

Tipton is a Black Country town in the Metropolitan Borough of Sandwell, between Dudley, West Bromwich and Wednesbury. Its postcode is DY4, and it is sometimes called the Venice of the Black Country because of the number of canals that pass through it. Sandwell Council is the local authority.

The specialist teams cover Tipton and its neighbourhoods, including Princes End, Great Bridge, Ocker Hill, Burnt Tree and Tipton Green. Describe the problem and give the postcode when you call.

Canals, locks and damp

The Birmingham Canal Navigations runs through Tipton in several levels, and the area has many locks, towpaths and canal-side housing. Water is part of the landscape, and so are the problems that come with it: damp, flooding in low-lying streets and old drains that struggle in heavy rain.

If water or sewage has entered a home, ask the team to dry it as well as clean it, and to take moisture readings. Our flood damage clean-up and sewage clean-up pages explain the process.

Terraces, estates and rented homes

Housing in Tipton ranges from Victorian terraces to inter-war and post-war estates and newer homes on cleared industrial land. There is a large private rented sector as well as housing association stock. Landlords regularly find that a departing tenant has left rubbish, human waste or sharps behind, or that a property has been used for a cannabis grow.

Ask for an itemised quote, photographs and waste paperwork. See our landlord and letting agent guide, and for former grows, cannabis farm clean-up.

Rail, tram and local contacts

Tipton has a railway station on the line between Birmingham and Wolverhampton, and the A4037 and A461 link it to Dudley and West Bromwich. West Midlands Police and West Midlands Fire Service cover the town.

Sandwell Council can advise about neglected and empty properties. If a property has been the scene of a crime or an unexplained death, cleaning can only start once the police have released it.

Dealing with damp and mould after the clean

In canal-side areas such as Tipton, cleaning is often only the start. If a property has been flooded or has suffered a long-term leak, the building needs drying and the mould needs treating, otherwise the problem returns within weeks. Ask the team to dry the property, take moisture readings before and after, and treat any mould they find.

For rented homes, tenants and landlords should know their rights and duties. Landlords must keep the property free of serious hazards, including damp and mould; tenants should report problems promptly. If the council needs to be involved, Sandwell Council's housing and environmental health teams can advise.

A common situation in Tipton

One regular scenario in this part of Sandwell is damp, mould and sewage after a leak or heavy rain, often in a rented home. The tenant reports it, the landlord arranges a plumber, and then everyone realises the contaminated materials are still there. A repair stops the leak but does not remove the contamination.

A specialist clean addresses what is left: removal of wet carpets and plasterboard, cleaning and disinfecting surfaces, drying the structure and treating mould. Ask for moisture readings before and after, and keep a record of every step. If the tenant is affected, the landlord should be told in writing, and the council's environmental health team can help if there is a dispute.

Who to contact in Tipton

For an emergency, always call 999. For a non-emergency police matter, the force covering Tipton is West Midlands Police, and the fire and rescue service is West Midlands Fire Service. The local authority is Sandwell Council, which handles environmental health, empty and neglected properties, and waste. The area uses the postcodes DY4, and nearby places include Princes End, Great Bridge, Dudley Port, Ocker Hill, Tividale, Coseley.

If a sudden death, crime or fire has happened, no cleaning can start until the emergency services have released the property. Once they have, call for an assessment.
