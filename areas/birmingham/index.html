Birmingham isn't one kind of place. A converted warehouse flat off Broad Street, a student house in Selly Oak, a Victorian terrace in Handsworth, a tower block in Castle Vale and a detached house in Sutton Coldfield each present a different problem when something goes wrong inside. The lift, the parking, the neighbours, the age of the floors and the landlord all change how a clean-up is done, how long it takes and who has to say yes.

This page is for people in Birmingham who need a biohazard clean and don't yet know where to begin. What happens when it's out of hours

Many Birmingham clean-ups start at awkward times: a death discovered on a Saturday, a flat found in a state on a Sunday evening, a sewage backup on a bank holiday. The coroner's office isn't open at those times, so the practical contact in the first hours is the police officer in charge or the funeral director. The call to make is the one that gets you an answer about whether the property has been released.

You don't have to wait for release to ring us. Calling early lets us understand the property, give you a figure and have a plan ready, so there's no avoidable delay when it is released. Keep out of the affected rooms until you've been told it's safe, and photograph from the doorway if you can.

How the type of property changes the job

Student houses and shared rentals

Selly Oak, Edgbaston, Moseley, Harborne and other areas with lots of shared houses and flats turn over their tenancies in tight windows. A property left in a poor state at the end of a tenancy needs work before the next group can move in, and there is often a deposit dispute running alongside. Dated photographs, an inventory and an itemised quote matter. Our landlords and letting agents page explains who needs to agree to the work and what paperwork to keep.

Flats, tower blocks and city-centre apartments

Floor number, lift size, loading access, concierge arrangements and the managing agent all affect the day. Tell us up front if the building has a bin store, a lift booking system or restricted parking, because waste has to come out of a building without disturbing anyone. Odour can pass through floors and shared corridors, so neighbours often notice before anyone has been told. A short, factual statement from the landlord or managing agent is better than leaving them guessing, and it doesn't need to include any detail about the people involved.

Victorian terraces and older houses

Older houses often have timber floors and gaps under skirting boards, which let fluids and odour travel further than they look. Carpet and underlay may be the visible problem, but floorboards and the void beneath them are often where the smell is held. If a clean-up is limited to the surface, the smell returns when the weather warms up. That is the main reason an honest quote for an older property sometimes includes lifting boards.

Larger houses and gardens

In places like Sutton Coldfield, properties tend to be bigger, with more rooms, outbuildings and garden areas affected. A hoarding case or a long-vacant house can involve sheds, garages and lofts. Tell us about them when you ring, because they change how many people and how many days are needed.

Commercial premises

Pubs, shops, offices, hostels and workshops can all need a biohazard clean. The difference is usually timing: the business wants to reopen, and a landlord, insurer or licensing authority may want a written record. Say how soon you need access, and we'll say what's realistic.

Water, drains and flooding

Birmingham has a lot of water running through it: the rivers Rea, Tame and Cole, and a canal network that is part of the city's character. Low-lying areas near watercourses can flood after heavy rain, and older drains can back up. Sewage from a public sewer is a matter for Severn Trent, while a blocked private drain is generally the property owner's concern. If you've had water in the house, sewage clean-up, flood damage clean up and flooded basement clean up go through what to do and who pays.

How to choose a company in Birmingham

A search will turn up directory listings, national franchises and local firms, and from a phone screen they look much the same. A few questions separate them.

  1. Is there a real local address and a person on the phone?Check the listing and the website agree.
  2. What does "24/7" mean?Ask what happens if you call at 10pm on a Sunday. Some listings advertise round-the-clock service but give office hours elsewhere.
  3. Is the quote itemised and in writing?A single figure with no breakdown is hard to compare or pass to an insurer.
  4. Who carries away the waste?Ask for the carrier's registration number. You can check it on the Environment Agency's public register.
  5. What paperwork will you get?Photos, a report of the work and waste records.
  6. Do they say what they won't do?Good teams tell you when something needs an electrician, a pest controller or a surveyor.
  7. Are they willing to say no?Sometimes the honest answer is that you don't need a specialist.

We aren't a substitute for your own checks. Ask us the same questions and compare the answers.

Birmingham biohazard cleaning questions

Do you cover the whole of Birmingham?

Yes, from the city centre to Sutton Coldfield and the districts in between. If your property is just outside the city boundary, give us the postcode and we'll tell you.

How quickly can someone come?

The phone line is open at all hours. Tell us it's urgent and what you've found, and we'll say how soon a team can attend. We don't promise specific times without knowing the job.

Can I clean up before the police or coroner have released the property?

No. Leave the scene as it is, and keep other people out. You can still ring to plan and get a quote.

Who is responsible for the clean-up in a rented flat?

Usually the landlord or their agent, although the estate or tenant may end up paying. Our guide on who cleans up after a death in a property explains how that works.

Will insurance pay?

Sometimes, depending on what happened and your policy. Read does home insurance cover biohazard cleaning before you decide whether to claim.

What if it's a high-rise flat?

Tell us the floor, the lift access and parking. These affect timing and waste removal.

Which council do I contact?

Birmingham City Council covers the city and Sutton Coldfield. Smethwick, Oldbury, Solihull and Wolverhampton are covered by other councils.

Can you help a landlord or agent with several properties?

Yes. Itemised quotes and before-and-after photos come back for each address.
