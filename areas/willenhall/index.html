Biohazard cleaning in Willenhall

Specialist clean-up for terraces, estates, flats, workshops and shops across Willenhall, WV12 and WV13, including Short Heath, New Invention, Portobello, Bentley and Lane Head. After a death, hoarding, rodents, sewage, or a tenancy that ended badly.

If your first question is "how much will this cost?", the answer is further down: what moves the price, what doesn't, and how to compare quotes without being caught out.

Willenhall is the second-largest town in Walsall borough, with nearly 50,000 people, and it has a reputation that most towns would envy: for centuries it was where Britain's locks and keys came from. That trade has largely gone, and what's left is a town of older terraces, estates built between and after the wars, workshops behind houses, and a modern edge around Bentley Bridge and the M6.

When people search for a biohazard cleaner here, they are usually in the middle of something stressful. They don't know what the job will cost, whether the first quote they get is fair, or who will be responsible for the bill. This page answers those questions first, and then covers the things that are particular to Willenhall: the council boundary that runs through Portobello, the old workshops and outbuildings, the green spaces beside the River Tame, and the town's new railway station. Phone or send an enquiry and a team is sourced for the address, with the price itemised and approved by you in writing before work starts. We arrange it; the team that attends does the cleaning.

How to compare quotes

Whoever you ring, these are fair questions:

  1. Is it in writing and itemised?A lump sum with no breakdown makes it hard to compare or to defend later.
  2. What exactly is included?Removal, cleaning, disinfection, odour treatment and waste disposal should each be clear.
  3. Is cleaning separate from clearance?Cleaning treats contamination; clearing empties rooms. A fair quote shows each.
  4. Who carries the waste, and will you get the paperwork?Ask for the transfer documents, which show it was moved lawfully.
  5. What happens if the scope changes on site?The answer should be a pause and a revised price you agree to, not a surprise bill.
  6. What records do you receive?Dated before-and-after photographs and a short report help with insurers, landlords and solicitors; the insurance guide explains why.
  7. Who is paying?An estate, a landlord, an insurer or a housing provider may have to approve the work first. Who pays for a hoarder clean up and who cleans up after a death in a property go through the common cases.

Walsall Council, with a bend through Portobello

Most of Willenhall is in Walsall borough, but when the old urban district was broken up in 1966, Portobello went to Wolverhampton. So two councils serve one town.

Old workshops and back yards

Lock-making was done in small workshops behind houses, and many Willenhall plots still have a rear building, a cellar or a yard. Whatever it is now, it needs to be included in the survey.

  • Tell us about every outbuilding, the cellar and the loft when you ring.
  • Don't disturb unknown materials. Old sheeting, lagging and stored chemicals can be hazardous. We'll say if something is beyond a biohazard clean and who you need.
  • Check the access. Rear entries and shared passages decide how waste leaves the property.

The Tame, the parks and the edge of town

Willenhall sits on the River Tame, and has green spaces including Willenhall Memorial Park, Short Heath Park and the Fibbersley Nature Trail and Reserve. Gardens near open ground and water attract rats; dumped rubbish at the edges can hide needles and glass. Treatment comes first, then cleaning; for sharps, read our needle sweep service page rather than picking anything up. For drain problems and backups, see sewage clean-up.

Landlords

Rented terraces and flats are common in the older parts of town. Photograph before anything is moved, keep the inventory and check-in report, and take advice on what you may do with belongings. The landlords and letting agents page lists useful records, and cannabis farm clean up covers properties used for growing.

Getting here: a new station and the M6

Willenhall and Darlaston have new railway stations that opened to passengers on 19 March 2026, the first rail service in those communities in more than 60 years. Junction 10 of the M6 is close, and the 529 bus links Walsall and Wolverhampton. For relatives arriving to meet the team or hand over keys, that makes travel simpler; for the team, the streets matter more, so tell us about parking, steps and shared entrances.

Willenhall biohazard cleaning questions

Do you cover Willenhall, Short Heath and New Invention?

Yes, along with Portobello, Bentley and Lane Head, plus nearby Bilston, Walsall and Wednesfield.

Which council covers Willenhall?

Walsall Council for most of the town, and City of Wolverhampton Council for Portobello.

Can you clear old workshops and outbuildings?

Yes, if we know about them in advance. Anything unidentified, such as sheeting or chemicals, is checked before it's touched.

Will I get a price before work starts?

Yes: an itemised written quote that you approve, and a revised one before any extra work.

Can landlords get photographs and paperwork?

Yes: dated before-and-after photos, an itemised bill and written confirmation of the waste removed.

I live too far away. Can you manage without me?

Yes. Keys can stay with someone you trust, and the paperwork is sent to you once the job is finished.
