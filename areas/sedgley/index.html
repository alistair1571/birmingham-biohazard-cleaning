Sedgley is a hill village that grew into a large suburb, and most of what we're asked to clean here is ordinary: a house that one person or couple lived in for decades, now unmanageable because of illness, a fall or a death. There are very few dramatic scenes. There is a lot of quiet difficulty, such as a kitchen nobody has been able to use, a bedroom that needs specialist treatment, or a family who live two hours away and don't know where to begin.

This page is written for them. It looks at the estates where most Sedgley homes sit, explains what the village's odd boundaries mean for councils and coroners, sets out the signs that a person living alone may need help, and describes what we do once you've decided to call. Call or send an enquiry and we find a team for the address; you see an itemised price and approve it in writing before anything starts. We coordinate, and whoever attends does the physical work.

The estates, and what long tenure leaves behind

Much of Sedgley was built in the second half of the twentieth century. The Northway estate, for example, grew from the mid-1950s into the 1970s and added about 1,000 houses, and others around it, such as Beacon, Straits, Sandyfields, High Arcal and Old Park Farm, filled in the ground between the old village and its neighbours. Many were bought or let to families who stayed.

That has a consequence for cleaning. Homes lived in by the same person for forty or fifty years tend to hold forty or fifty years of belongings, and the gap between "nobody has been in for a while" and "we now need a specialist" can be short.

Worried about someone? What to do before you clean anything

Neighbours and relatives are often the first to notice. These are signs worth acting on:

  • Post, milk or bins left out for days
  • Curtains that haven't moved, or lights that stay on or off
  • A smell from the property, or flies at the windows
  • A person who stops answering the phone or the door

What to do depends on how worried you are:

  1. If you think someone is in danger,call 999. Don't force your way in.
  2. If you're concerned but it isn't an emergency,ring 101 and ask for a welfare check. Say what you've seen and when.
  3. If you know the person has been struggling,their GP or the adult social care team at Dudley Council can offer support. Dudley Council Plus is on 0300 555 2345.
  4. If it's a rented home,tell the landlord or housing officer.

Cleaning comes afterwards. A house should never be cleared while someone is still living in it unless they agree and understand what's happening, or you hold legal authority to act for them.

Where does Sedgley stop? Councils, coroners and boundaries

The old Manor of Sedgley covered nine villages, among them Gornal, Woodsetton, Coseley and Ettingshall, and the boundaries were redrawn more than once. In 1966 most of the area joined Dudley, while Goldthorn Park went to Wolverhampton and Gospel End to a rural district in Staffordshire. So an address that feels like Sedgley can belong to a different council or force.

  • Sedgley, Gornal, Woodsetton and nearby DY3 addresses: Dudley Council covers housing, environmental health and rubbish, and the Black Country Coroner Service deals with a sudden or unexplained death.
  • Goldthorn Park and addresses on the Wolverhampton side: a different council applies, and your council tax bill will say which.
  • Gospel End and the Staffordshire edge: check the bill, and ask whoever is dealing with a death which coroner has the case.

Where a neighbouring property is the problem, with rubbish, rats or a smell, Dudley Council can act on private land where waste attracts vermin. Our Brierley Hill page explains what the council's rodent service covers and what it doesn't.

Hills, steps and where the van stops

Sedgley sits on the hill, with Beacon Hill at 237 metres one of the highest points in the West Midlands. Access varies a great deal. Estate semis may have a drive and a short carry, while older streets near the centre have steps, narrow pavements and limited parking. Tell us:

  • how many steps lead to the door
  • where a van can park and how far that is
  • whether the property is a flat, a bungalow or a house with a steep garden

The village centre: pubs, shops and flats above

Sedgley's centre, around the Bull Ring and High Street, has older buildings and pubs, shops with flats above, and the usual shared stairs and doors. Smell, pests and sewage problems there affect neighbours quickly, so speed and discretion matter. Tell us who holds the keys, when the premises trade and how waste can leave the building. For sewage, see sewage clean-up, and for smoke or fire, fire damage cleaning.

Green edges: Baggeridge, Cotwall End and the Beacon

Sedgley has a lot of open ground close to housing. Baggeridge Country Park sits on a former colliery site, and Cotwall End Valley and Beacon Hill are nearby. Gardens that back onto these places see more rats, foxes and dumped rubbish than a street in the middle of a town. Rats and mice should be dealt with by a pest controller before cleaning begins, and anything sharp found in undergrowth or fly-tipping should be left alone; our needle sweep service page explains how to handle it.

Doing it for a family: authority, records and cost

  • Authority. Executors, attorneys and owners can approve work; a relative without authority cannot. The blog on who cleans up after a death in a property shows how that varies by tenancy.
  • Keepsakes. Tell us what to set aside before anyone starts: documents, jewellery, photographs, medication.
  • Records. Dated before-and-after photos and a short report are what a solicitor, landlord or insurer will ask for. The insurance guide explains when cover applies.
  • Cost. Price follows the volume to be removed, the type of contamination, how long it has been left, the carry from door to van and how quickly you need it done. If anything changes on site, work pauses until you've agreed the new figure.

Sedgley biohazard cleaning questions

Do you cover Sedgley, Gornal and Coseley?

Yes, along with Woodsetton, Straits and Gospel End, plus nearby Dudley, Bilston, Kingswinford and Wolverhampton.

Is Sedgley in Dudley or Wolverhampton?

Dudley borough, although it borders Wolverhampton and some nearby addresses are in a different council area.

Can you clean a house after a death?

Yes: assess the property, remove what can't be saved, disinfect and treat the smell where it starts. Items to keep can be set aside first.

What if my relative is still alive but the house is unsafe?

Their agreement matters, or the authority of an attorney. A GP or adult social care can help the person, and we deal with the property when they or their representative are ready.

I live too far away. Can you manage without me?

Yes. Keys can stay with a neighbour or solicitor, and the paperwork is sent to you when it's done.

Is cleaning the same as clearing?

No. Cleaning removes contamination, while clearing empties the property, and they should be priced on separate lines.
