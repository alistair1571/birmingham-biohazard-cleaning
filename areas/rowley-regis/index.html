Rowley Regis is not one place. The name covers a cluster of towns on and around the Rowley Hills, with about 50,000 people between Blackheath, Cradley Heath, Old Hill, Rowley Village and Tividale, and most callers describe their street by the town name, not by the one on the council's map. That matters when you phone a cleaning company, because the first thing a good one needs is the right road, the right type of building and the right person to approve the work.

This page is organised around what people actually ask when they ring from here: which of the towns they're in, what to do before they open the door, what's likely to be behind the house as well as in it, and what happens when the property is a hillside terrace that a van can't reach. Get in touch and we line up a team for the address, with each cost written down and agreed before anyone starts. We arrange the work; the people who attend do it.

Whichever you're in, the postcode is B65 for most of it, and your council tax bill confirms which council applies. Most of the area is in Sandwell, but when the old Rowley Regis borough was broken up in 1966, parts went to Dudley and Halesowen, so a house near an edge can belong to a different council from the one next door. In an emergency call 999, and use 101 to ask when police will release a property after a death or an incident.

Your first visit: ten minutes that save money

If you hold the keys to a house you haven't seen in a while, this is the order we'd suggest. Nothing here involves cleaning anything.

  1. Tell someone where you're going,and don't go alone if you expect something serious.
  2. Check who has authority.Executor, attorney, landlord or the person living there? Without it, don't move belongings.
  3. Open the door and stop.A strong smell, flies, a fridge door left open or a floor that gives underfoot are all reasons to step back.
  4. Photograph from the doorway,with dates, and each room as you reach it.
  5. Don't touch sharps, droppings or fluids.Wear gloves if you must handle a door or switch, and wash your hands afterwards.
  6. Leave windows and doors as you found themif there has been a death, until the police or coroner's officer has said the property is released.
  7. Note what must not be lost:papers, jewellery, photographs, medication, keys.
  8. Ring us with what you saw.Photos plus a short description are usually enough for a first view and a rough scope.

If it is a rented property, add one step before the rest: check the tenancy position. Our landlords and letting agents page explains what paperwork to keep.

Behind the terrace: workshops, brewhouses and yards

Cradley Heath was one of Britain's centres of chain-making, and nail- and chain-making in this part of the Black Country was often done in small workshops behind people's houses. Many of those yards and outbuildings survive in one form or another: converted to garages, used as stores, left to rot or built over. For a clean-up, that history has two practical effects.

  • The house is often only half the property. Rear outbuildings, cellars and yards behind older terraces collect belongings, damp and rodents. Tell us what is out the back, and whether it is accessible only through the house.
  • Shared access is common. Passages, entries and shared yards mean neighbours may see and smell what's happening. A short, courteous word with them at the start usually goes a long way.
  • Stored items may be old or unknown. If a shed holds paint, solvents, asbestos sheeting or anything you can't identify, don't disturb it. We'll say if a job is beyond a biohazard clean and who you need.

For homes packed with belongings, see hoarders clean up and who pays for a hoarder clean up, which explain the staged approach and how costs fall.

Hillsides, steps and the carry

Rowley Regis is built on hills, and Turner's Hill, at 269 metres, is the highest point in the West Midlands region. For a cleaning team that mostly means one thing: stairs and slopes between the front door and the van.

  • Tell us how many steps lead to the door, and whether the pavement is narrow or the street one-way.
  • Say where a van can stop and how far that is from the property.
  • Mention a shared entrance, a lift, or an upstairs bedroom.

These details can move a quote more than the contamination does, because every item is carried by hand. Photos of the approach help as much as photos of the room.

Quarries, hill edges and open ground

Quarrying Rowley Rag, a hard dolerite, went on for around 200 years until the last quarry closed in 2008. Mining and brick-making came earlier, and the ground and edges around them are now a mix of woodland, wasteland and grassland close to houses.

  • Fly-tipping. Edge land and lanes collect dumped rubbish that may conceal needles, nappies, food waste or broken glass. Keep children and dogs away and report it to whoever owns the land. See needle sweep service.
  • Rats and mice. Gardens, sheds and garages backing onto open ground are classic locations. Pest control comes first, then cleaning. Sandwell Council's own team treats rodents; our Oldbury page sets out how that service works, and after rodent clean up covers the clean-up.
  • Drains and surface water. Steep ground sheds water fast, and it can overwhelm low drains. See sewage clean-up and flood damage clean up.

Relatives arriving by train

Rowley Regis and Cradley Heath both have railway stations on the line between Birmingham and Stourbridge or Worcester, and the Rowley Regis station has been open since 1867. Relatives and executors often travel in from other cities, collect the keys, look round and leave. If that's you:

  • Meet us, or a neighbour or solicitor holding the keys, at an agreed time, or give us access arrangements by phone.
  • Choose what must be kept before you travel, and tell us where it is.
  • Ask for dated photos and a short summary to be emailed afterwards; a solicitor, landlord or insurer will want the same.

After a death the order is different: after death cleaning explains the work, and our post on who cleans up after a death in a property explains who pays. A sudden death in this part of Sandwell goes to the Black Country Coroner Service in nearby Oldbury, and the house stays off limits to cleaners until it is handed back.

Landlords and agents in B65

Much of the area's older housing is private rented, and the pattern of calls is familiar: needles, human waste, rubbish, a tenant who has stopped coping, or a house with signs of cannabis growing; see cannabis farm clean up. Photograph before anything is touched, keep the inventory, and check what you can lawfully do with belongings. The insurance guide explains why the cause of damage matters to a claim.

What changes the price

The main drivers are the amount of material to be removed, the type of contamination, how far it has spread, the carry from door to van, how many outbuildings are involved and how soon you need it done. If the scope changes on site, work pauses until you've agreed the new figure.

Rowley Regis biohazard cleaning questions

Do you cover Blackheath, Cradley Heath and Old Hill?

Yes, along with Rowley Village and Tividale, plus nearby Halesowen, Oldbury, Dudley and West Bromwich.

Which council is Rowley Regis in?

Mostly Sandwell Council, but some edges belong to Dudley or Halesowen, so use your council tax bill.

Can you work on steep streets with no parking outside?

Yes. Tell us about steps, slopes and where a van can stop, and the carry is planned and priced in advance.

Can you clear sheds and rear yards as well as the house?

Yes, though anything you can't identify, such as paint, solvents or old sheeting, needs checking first.

I live in another city. How does it work?

A relative, neighbour or solicitor can meet the team or hold the keys, and the paperwork follows by email.

Is cleaning the same as clearing the house?

No. Cleaning treats contamination, and clearing empties the property. Ask for them to be priced separately.
