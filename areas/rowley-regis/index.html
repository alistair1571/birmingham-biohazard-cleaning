Biohazard cleaning in Rowley Regis, B65

Rowley Regis is made up of several communities in the Metropolitan Borough of Sandwell, including Blackheath, Cradley Heath, Old Hill and Rowley Village. Its postcode is B65, and it lies between Dudley, Halesowen and Oldbury on the high ground of the Rowley Hills. Because the local names are so varied, an address may be described under any of them. All are covered.

Give the postcode and a short description when you call. The team will tell you what they need to know and arrange an assessment.

Chain-making towns on steep hills

Cradley Heath was the centre of the British chain-making industry, and the Women Chainmakers' strike of 1910 is part of local history. Rowley Regis itself was known for stone: the dark dolerite of the Rowley Hills, sometimes called Rowley Rag, was quarried for road stone. Much of the area was built on slopes, so terraced streets climb steeply.

Steep streets make access harder. Steps up to front doors, narrow pavements and limited parking affect how waste is carried out and where a van can park. Mention these details when you book so the quote and the plan are accurate.

Mixed housing and private lets

Homes here range from Victorian terraces to inter-war council houses and post-war estates. Private landlords own many of the terraces, and housing associations manage many of the flats. As in neighbouring towns, landlords and agents regularly need contaminated or neglected properties cleaned and made lettable, with records to support a deposit claim.

Our guide for landlords and letting agents explains what a reliable clean-up includes, and our cost guide shows how prices are worked out.

Trains, council and emergency services

Rowley Regis station and Cradley Heath station are on the Birmingham to Worcester and Stourbridge line, with direct trains into Birmingham. The teams travel by road. Sandwell Council is the local authority for Rowley Regis, Blackheath and Old Hill. Neighbouring Cradley and Halesowen are across the border in Dudley, so if you are unsure which council applies to an address, check the council tax bill.

West Midlands Police and West Midlands Fire Service cover the area. For concerns about a neighbour's home, Sandwell Council can advise, and anyone can request a Safe and Well visit from the fire service.

Hills, access and parking

Rowley Regis is hilly, and some streets are steep and narrow with limited parking. That is a practical problem for any clean-up: waste has to be moved in sealed containers, and the van may be some way from the door. Tell the team about steps, gradients and parking restrictions when you book.

If you are a landlord or an agent with several properties on the hills, ask the team to plan an efficient route and consider whether tasks such as clearance and cleaning can be done in the same visit. That can reduce costs and the time a property sits empty.

A common situation in Rowley Regis

On the steep streets around Blackheath and Old Hill, access can be the main issue. A family needs a terraced house cleared after a bereavement, but the van cannot park nearby and the house has steep steps and a narrow hallway. Waste has to be carried a long way.

Tell the team all this when you ring. They can allow extra time, bring more people and arrange for a van to wait on a nearby road. The quote will reflect the extra effort, and you will avoid surprises on the day.
