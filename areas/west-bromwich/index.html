West Bromwich is the biggest town in Sandwell, with about 100,000 people in the built-up area, and it is two towns in one. The town centre is a retail and civic hub, with two shopping centres from 1971, a newer Tesco-anchored scheme from 2013, an arts centre, a college campus and a hospital close by. Around it are the estates and terraces where most people live, and on the edges are parks, a country park and the tail of the Great Barr side.

The town centre and commercial premises

West Bromwich's centre has seen shops come and go: Marks and Spencer closed in 1990, Littlewoods in 2005 and Woolworths in 2008, and new schemes have replaced older ones. Empty units and units above shops carry their own risks.

  • Sewage and drain backups in food premises. Stop trading in the affected area, keep customers and staff out, and speak to Sandwell Council's environmental health team before reopening. The clean-up is described under sewage clean-up.
  • Needles and human waste in doorways and service yards. Sharps should be left for a trained team; the needle sweep service and human waste cleaning pages explain the process.
  • Pigeons. Roosts on roofs and ledges leave droppings that shouldn't be swept dry; see pigeon guano cleaning.
  • Flats above shops. Shared stairs, bin stores and trading hours mean the work has to be planned around neighbours.
  • Out-of-hours work. Evenings and early mornings avoid lost trade, though they cost more.
  • Match days. West Bromwich Albion play at The Hawthorns on the edge of the centre, and home match days affect roads and parking. Mention the date when you book so the van isn't stuck.

On any commercial job, ask for dated photos, a summary of what was done and the waste paperwork; a landlord, insurer or inspector may want all three later.

After a cannabis grow

A cannabis grow found in a rented West Bromwich property usually involves the police first, then the landlord, then us. The order matters.

  1. Police first.Don't go in until officers have released the property. They may need to remove plants and equipment.
  2. Then safety.Illegal grows often involve bypassed or altered electrics. An electrician should check the supply before anything is switched on.
  3. Then the building.High humidity for months leaves mould, damp and contaminated carpet and plasterboard. These are removed and the surfaces treated.
  4. Then the paperwork.Landlords need photos, an itemised bill and dates for the insurer, the letting agent and possibly the tenant's deposit scheme.

Read cannabis farm clean up for what the work involves, and our blog on what to do if you find a cannabis farm in your rental property for the landlord's side. The insurance guide explains why the cause of damage matters to a claim.

Family homes and estates

West Bromwich's council housing goes back to 1920, with the Tantany Estate. The Harvills Hawthorn Estate, completed in 1948, was the first major postwar council development, and large interwar and postwar estates such as Charlemont Farm and Friar Park followed. Many of the homes are now owner-occupied, rented privately or held by housing providers.

  • A long-term owner has died or been taken ill. Authority comes first, and our post on who cleans up after a death in a property shows how it varies.
  • A house full of belongings. Go in stages; hoarders clean up explains how, and who pays for a hoarder clean up covers the bill.
  • A home near Sandwell General Hospital. A hospital stay can mean relatives see a house for the first time in months. Deal with safety first and put keepsakes aside.
  • A death at home. The work is described under after death cleaning; a sudden death goes to the Black Country Coroner Service in Oldbury.

Postcode and boundary traps

West Bromwich's postcodes are B70 and B71, with parts of WS5 and WS10 as well, and its boundaries were redrawn in 1966 when it took in most of Tipton and Wednesbury. Friar Park straddles the line with Wednesbury, so the road name matters more than the town name.

  • West Bromwich, Tipton and Wednesbury: Sandwell Council. Its pest team treats rats and mice and charges for indoor visits; see our Oldbury page.
  • WS10 addresses: shared with Darlaston, which is in Walsall; our Wednesbury page explains why that matters.
  • Great Barr (B43): incorporated into the borough in 1888 and treated as Sandwell, but check your council tax bill if you're near the edge.

Parks, the valley and the edge of town

Sandwell Valley Country Park covers about 660 acres, and Dartmouth Park has been open since 1878. Properties beside open ground see more foxes, rats and dumped rubbish than those in the town's centre. Gardens that back onto parkland, and sheds and garages in particular, are common sites for droppings; treat the animals first, then clean. See after rodent clean up.

Landlords: the evidence that helps

Photograph before anything moves, hold on to the inventory and check-in report, and take advice on belongings. What else to keep is set out on the landlords and letting agents page.

Who to contact, and which council

For housing problems, rubbish, empty properties and food-premises issues, the council is Sandwell. Dial 999 in an emergency, and 101 to ask police when a property will be handed back; nothing is cleaned after a sudden death, crime or fire until it is.

What changes the price

Cost reflects volume, contamination type, time since the event, access and urgency. Commercial and out-of-hours jobs cost more, and extra work is only done after you've agreed a revised figure.
