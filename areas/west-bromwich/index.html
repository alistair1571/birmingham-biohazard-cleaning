Biohazard cleaning in West Bromwich, B70 and B71

West Bromwich is the administrative centre of the Metropolitan Borough of Sandwell, with the council headquarters in the town, and it sits between Birmingham and Wolverhampton close to junction 1 of the M5. Its postcodes are B70 and B71, with B43 on the Great Barr side. Sandwell Council is the local authority.

The specialist teams cover West Bromwich and its neighbours, including Oldbury, Tipton, Wednesbury and Smethwick. Give the postcode when you call and describe what has happened.

Football, parks and a busy town centre

The town is best known for West Bromwich Albion, whose ground, The Hawthorns, lies on the edge of the centre. Dartmouth Park, Sandwell Valley and Kenrick Park provide green space, and the West Midlands Metro runs through the centre at West Bromwich Central and other stops.

Match days bring crowds, and the town centre has shops, bars and offices. Licensed premises and landlords of flats above shops can face urgent clean-ups of vomit, bodily fluids and needles. Ask for out-of-hours attendance and photographs of the finished work.

Terraces, estates and the private rented sector

Housing includes Victorian terraces around Greets Green and Hateley Heath, inter-war estates and post-war housing at Charlemont and Friar Park, with newer developments on cleared industrial land. A significant part of the older stock is privately rented.

Landlords and agents regularly need tenancy clear-outs, hoarded properties made safe and former cannabis grows stripped and cleaned. See our landlord guide and cannabis farm clean-up pages.

The Oak House and old West Bromwich

West Bromwich keeps a few reminders of its past, including the timber-framed Oak House, a Tudor manor house now run as a museum. Old buildings and listed properties often have fragile finishes and awkward access, so tell the team in advance.

West Midlands Police and West Midlands Fire Service cover the town. If a death is unexplained, cleaning can only begin once the police have released the property; the team can advise on next steps once it is safe to enter.

Match days, events and timing

On Albion match days, parking and traffic around the Hawthorns can be difficult. If your property is nearby, mention it when booking so the team can allow extra time or choose a different window.

The same applies to events and market days in the town centre. For commercial customers, plan the work around opening hours and ask for out-of-hours attendance if needed. The team should confirm the arrival window in advance and tell you if anything delays them.

A common situation in West Bromwich

Landlords with flats and houses in the town regularly meet the same problem at the end of a tenancy: a property that was let in good order but returned with rubbish, food waste, damp or human waste. The usual instinct is to book a general cleaner and a skip. That approach can go wrong when waste includes sharps, soiled bedding or mould, since general cleaners are not trained or insured to handle it.

A specialist team can sort, remove and dispose of contaminated waste lawfully, then disinfect and deodorise. They can provide the paperwork landlords need, including a waste transfer note, dated photographs and an itemised invoice. That documentation supports a deposit claim and an insurance claim.

Who to contact in West Bromwich

For an emergency, always call 999. For a non-emergency police matter, the force covering West Bromwich is West Midlands Police, and the fire and rescue service is West Midlands Fire Service. The local authority is Sandwell Council, which handles environmental health, empty and neglected properties, and waste. The area uses the postcodes B70 and B71, and nearby places include Hateley Heath, Greets Green, Charlemont, Great Barr, Oldbury, Wednesbury.

If a sudden death, crime or fire has happened, no cleaning can start until the emergency services have released the property. Once they have, call for an assessment.
