Biohazard cleaning in Brierley Hill

Specialist clean-up for homes, flats, rentals and commercial units across Brierley Hill and DY5, from Hawbush, Chapel Street and Withymoor to the Merry Hill and Waterfront area. After a death, hoarding, sewage, rats, pigeons, needles or a tenancy left in a state.

Brierley Hill is a town with two faces sharing one postcode. On one side are streets of older houses, 1960s council flats where Victorian terraces once stood, and estates that went up in the 1920s and again in the 1970s and 1980s. On the other side is Merry Hill, one of the largest shopping centres in the country, with the Waterfront offices on the site of the old Round Oak steelworks, and the retail parks, restaurants, car parks and loading bays that go with them.

That mix gives us two kinds of caller: householders, relatives and landlords dealing with a property, and business owners and managers with a unit or site that has to reopen. Below, you'll find how biohazard cleaning plays out for each, which Dudley bodies get involved, which pests the council will and won't treat, and what to gather before you call. A phone call or enquiry gets a specialist team lined up and a written, itemised price agreed before work begins. We do the coordinating; the team who attend do the cleaning.

Housing built on old Brierley Hill

The town's housing reflects its history. Iron works, collieries and glassworks defined it, then closed, and the land was rebuilt in layers.

  • Older estates such as Hawbush, built in the late 1920s and early 1930s, are now often in their second or third generation of ownership. When an owner dies or goes into care, relatives can face a house full of decades of belongings. See hoarders clean up, and the blog post who pays for a hoarder clean up.
  • The Chapel Street area, where 1960s council flats replaced Victorian terraces. Flats mean shared stairs, bin stores and neighbours, so smell and access need planning. Social tenants should tell their landlord first.
  • Withymoor Village and Lakeside, developed in the 1970s and 1980s on land that had been opencast coal workings. These are family estates of houses, many now privately let.
  • Private lets. Landlords who live elsewhere often find out about a problem late. A dated inventory and regular inspections make a difference when a tenancy ends badly. See our landlords and letting agents page.

If someone has died at home, nothing can be cleaned until the property is released. Our after death cleaning page explains the sequence, and the blog guide to who cleans up after a death in a property sets out who is responsible in each kind of home.

Merry Hill, the Waterfront and commercial units

Retail and office space in this part of DY5 creates a different set of problems.

  • Restaurants, cafés and food units. Sewage backing up, grease-trap failures and rodent activity can close a kitchen. If you prepare food, check with Dudley Council's environmental health team before reopening after sewage or pests. See sewage clean-up and after rodent clean up.
  • Loading bays, car parks and service yards. Needles, human waste and fly-tipped rubbish collect in quiet corners. Don't pick up sharps by hand. Our needle sweep service page explains safe handling.
  • Pigeons. Large roofs, canopies and open-sided structures make ideal roosts, and droppings build up on ledges and in gutters. Dry droppings shouldn't be swept. See pigeon guano cleaning. The council doesn't treat pigeons, so a pest specialist is the route for proofing.
  • Empty units. Vacant shops and offices still attract waste, pests and break-ins. Tell us if a unit is empty so access and security can be arranged.
  • After a break-in, fire or flood. See fire damage cleaning and flood damage clean up.
  • Working around trade. Evenings and early mornings are often the only way to clean without losing customers, at a premium. Tell us when you must be open again.
  • Landlord, tenant and insurer. Check the lease and tell your insurer before agreeing work you may want to claim for. Our guide on whether insurance covers biohazard cleaning explains how claims usually turn on the cause.

Tell us what the unit is used for, who runs it, and how the team will get in and out. Those three answers shape the plan.

Rats, mice and what Dudley Council will do

This is where Dudley differs from some neighbouring boroughs. Dudley Council says its pest control service is provided by a British Pest Control Association member and covers properties within the borough on weekdays, and at the time of writing it was only accepting requests for rats and mice. It says it does not treat pigeons, squirrels, bees, foxes and some other animals, though it gives advice. Charges are on the council's fees page, so check them there.

What that means in practice:

  • Treat first, clean second. Cleaning a loft or kitchen while rats are still active is wasted money. Get the pest control done, then call us for the contamination. See after rodent clean up.
  • Droppings and nests need proper handling. Contaminated insulation, cables and food-handling areas are cleaned and disinfected, and what can't be saved is removed.
  • Neighbouring rubbish. Councils can serve notices where waste attracts rats. In one Dudley case, a father and son were fined after failing to comply with a notice under the Prevention of Damage by Pests Act 1949 over waste piled at a rented property. If a neighbour's property is the problem, contact Dudley Council Plus on 0300 555 2345.
  • Pigeons and squirrels. The council doesn't treat them, so you'll need a private pest controller.

Who to contact in Brierley Hill

Emergencies go to 999. For anything calmer, including finding out when police will hand a property back, use 101. Housing, environmental health, pest control requests and rubbish complaints in DY5 all sit with Dudley Council. If a death was sudden or unexplained, the Black Country Coroner Service deals with it from the Coroner's Court in Oldbury; it also covers Sandwell, Walsall and Wolverhampton and is separate from the Birmingham and Solihull service. Severn Trent owns the public sewers, whereas a blocked drain inside your boundary is usually yours to sort. Renters should tell their landlord or housing provider, and anyone planning a claim should speak to their insurer before booking.

Canals, old workings and flooding

Brierley Hill grew up around canals, quarries and mines, and much of the land has been built on more than once. Canalside redevelopment, such as the housing proposed on the former factory site at Canal Street, is changing the area. For homeowners, the practical points are damp, occasional flooding after heavy rain and older drains. If water has reached your home, see flooded basement clean up and flood damage clean up.

Planning ahead: access and parking

The A461 and the retail areas around Merry Hill can be busy, and a team arriving with equipment needs somewhere to park. Plans for a Metro extension may change travel through the area in future. Useful things to mention when you ring: where a van can stop, whether there are stairs, lifts or shared doors, who has the keys and when, whether the utilities are on, and your trading hours if it's a business.

What affects the price

In DY5 the biggest swings come from three things: whether the job is a flat, a house or a retail unit; how long the problem has been left; and how easy it is to get people and waste in and out. Sharps, bodily fluids and rodent contamination add protective work. Evening and weekend slots cost more, which matters for shops. If a landlord, estate or centre manager has to sign the quote off, factor in their time.

Send photos and a few lines and you'll typically get an opening figure quickly; the final price is confirmed in writing before anyone starts.

Brierley Hill biohazard cleaning questions

Do you cover Brierley Hill and the Merry Hill area?

Yes, including the Waterfront, Round Oak, Hawbush, Chapel Street, Withymoor and Lakeside, and nearby Pensnett, Quarry Bank, Kingswinford and Cradley Heath. Give us your postcode if you're unsure.

Can you clean a retail or office unit?

Yes. Tell us the use, the access and your opening times so work can be planned around them.

Which council do I contact?

Dudley Council, as Brierley Hill is in the Metropolitan Borough of Dudley.

Does Dudley Council deal with rats?

It says it does, through a pest control contractor, though it was only taking requests for rats and mice when we looked. Check its page for current charges.

Can you help after a tenant leaves a flat contaminated?

Yes. Say who the landlord or agent is and who can approve spending. Don't throw out the tenant's belongings or change locks without taking advice first.

Do I have to be at the property?

Not at all. A relative, neighbour or agent can hold the keys.

Can you work around shop opening hours?

Often, though evening and weekend work costs more. Give us your deadline.
