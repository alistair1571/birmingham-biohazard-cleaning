Biohazard cleaning in Brierley Hill, DY5

Brierley Hill sits about three miles south of Dudley and a mile north of Stourbridge on the A461, in the Dudley borough. Around 14,000 people lived here at the 2011 census. The town is best known for glass and steel, and today for the Merry Hill Shopping Centre, which lies immediately to the east and is one of the largest in the UK.

You can reach the same specialist teams from anywhere in DY5, and from neighbouring Kingswinford, Pensnett, Quarry Bank, Netherton and Amblecote. Give the postcode and describe the problem, and the team will explain what happens next.

Housing built on top of old Brierley Hill

The town's housing reflects its past. Chapel Street Estate was built in the 1960s, mostly as council flats, on the site of Victorian terraces. Hawbush Estate dates from the late 1920s and early 1930s. Withymoor Village and Lakeside were mostly developed in the 1970s and 1980s after opencast coal mining. In each of those areas, ownership has changed hands many times, and properties range from well-kept family homes to flats that have been neglected.

Typical jobs include hoarded or neglected flats, tenancy handovers where a tenant has left a property contaminated, and clean-ups after a death. Our hoarder clean up and after-death cleaning pages explain what each involves.

Merry Hill, Waterfront and commercial units

Merry Hill was built between 1985 and 1989 on former farmland, and the Waterfront office complex followed on the former Round Oak steelworks site between 1989 and 1995. Large retail and office developments have their own needs: sewage backups in toilets, leaks from roof and upper floors, pigeon droppings under canopies and on ledges, and needles in car parks and service areas.

Commercial customers should ask about out-of-hours attendance, a written quote and waste documentation, and about keeping public areas closed off during the work. Empty units, which can be affected by pigeons and vandalism, are covered as well.

Rail, tram and planning ahead

The nearest railway station is Cradley Heath, over a mile away, and a West Midlands Metro extension north of Brierley Hill is planned. In practice, access is by road and bus, with the A461 bypass running behind the Asda store.

Dudley Council is the local authority for Brierley Hill, and West Midlands Police and West Midlands Fire Service cover the area. If you are worried about a neighbour's hoarded home, the fire service offers Safe and Well visits that anyone can refer someone for, and Dudley Council can advise about neglected properties.

Commercial and residential in the same postcode

Brierley Hill is unusual in having a huge retail and office district a short distance from older residential streets. A team that works in both should be able to explain the difference: in homes, the priority is discretion and care with belongings; in commercial premises, the priority is speed, documentation and keeping the public away from the work.

If you manage a unit near Merry Hill, ask whether the team can work out of hours, whether they carry public liability insurance and whether they can supply a written method statement. For homes in DY5, ask the same questions about insurance and waste carrier registration, and ask for a quote that lists exactly what is included.

A common situation in Brierley Hill

A facilities manager at a retail unit receives a report of a foul smell in a service corridor. A drain has failed, sewage has pooled under a store room, and staff are being sent home. The company needs the area cleared and the premises open quickly.

A specialist team can attend quickly, extract and clean, and document their work with photographs and waste transfer notes. Ask for a written summary for your insurer and for the landlord, and make sure the drainage fault is repaired before the area reopens.

Who to contact in Brierley Hill

For an emergency, always call 999. For a non-emergency police matter, the force covering Brierley Hill is West Midlands Police, and the fire and rescue service is West Midlands Fire Service. The local authority is Dudley Council, which handles environmental health, empty and neglected properties, and waste. The area uses the postcodes DY5, and nearby places include Pensnett, Quarry Bank, Merry Hill, Kingswinford, Netherton, Amblecote.
